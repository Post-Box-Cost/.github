## What is Postbox?

**Postbox** is a powerful, borderless email client designed for professionals and power users who demand efficiency and seamless multitasking. Unlike traditional email applications that can be disruptive when switching between tasks, Postbox offers a **borderless windowed experience**, allowing you to manage your emails smoothly while working in other applications.

With Postbox, you can enjoy:
- **Seamless alt-tab functionality** for quick switching between email and other apps.
- A **stable, non-minimizing window** that stays responsive.
- **Enhanced productivity** with advanced email management features.
- Perfect integration for **streamers, professionals, and multitaskers**.

Postbox supports all major email services and is optimized for workflows that require constant app switching. Its clean, customizable interface and robust performance make it the ideal choice for anyone looking to streamline their email management without interruptions.
